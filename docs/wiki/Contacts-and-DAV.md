# Contacts and DAV

Contacts support standard vCard/CardDAV fields including names, email addresses, phones, organisation, notes, birthday (`BDAY`) and anniversary (`ANNIVERSARY`). Date fields use `YYYY-MM-DD` and appear in the read-only Contact dates calendar.

DAV clients must use revocable application passwords. Inboxora primary-login credentials are not DAV credentials. Read-only CardDAV imports cannot be edited locally.

Address books appear as scrollable tabs above search on every device. **All visible** retains the visibility filter; a hidden book can still be selected explicitly. The adjacent menu provides book creation, visibility, Google CSV import into local books, and Google CSV/Outlook CSV/vCard export. All existing contact fields remain available in the editor.

Wide screens show the list and contact details side by side. On tablets and phones, selecting a contact opens its details with an in-app Back action. Selecting an email address opens Inboxora's composer with that recipient.

Switching address-book tabs retains the current search. Responses and pagination from a previously selected book cannot replace or append to the current results.
