# Inboxora Roadmap

The project roadmap lives on the website and is mirrored here for active fork work.

It follows a **Now / Next / Later** format — no dates, no commitments. Priorities shift as the community's needs become clear.

## Now

- **In progress:** dedicated, revocable DAV application passwords plus first-party contacts and calendar resources, so CardDAV and CalDAV sync through DAVx5 works cleanly for password, TOTP and OIDC accounts.
- **In progress:** Contacts and Calendar as first-class destinations in the left sidebar; external CalDAV and iCalendar sources will be read-only initially.

Have a feature idea? [Open an issue](https://github.com/Dragonk/Inboxora/issues).
